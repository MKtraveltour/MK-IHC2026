Remarks & Cancellation Policy
注意事項・キャンセル規定

  • Reservation is not complete until MK sends you a confirmation with your reservation number.
  • Please book by 5:00 PM (JST), at least 4 days prior to your travel date.
  • We can only accept reservations where either the pick-up or drop-off is within Kyoto City.
  • This service is direct only — one pick-up point to the airport, with no stops along the way.
  • The highway toll / parking fee shown in your fare estimate is a fixed amount, settled by non-refundable advance payment — it will not vary based on the actual cost on the day.
    高速料金・駐車料金は事前決済の固定額となり、当日の実費による変動はございません。
  • A late-night / early-morning surcharge of 20% applies between 22:00 and 5:00.
    深夜早朝（22:00〜5:00）は2割増しとなります。
  • A parking fee is charged for airport pick-up only (Kansai → Kyoto, Itami → Kyoto): ¥1,000 at Kansai, ¥300 at Itami.
    空港お迎え時は駐車料金として関空¥1,000、伊丹¥300を別途頂戴いたします。
  • The exact vehicle model within your matched class (e.g. which minivan, Alphard or Vellfire) cannot be requested and is assigned based on availability.
    ミニバン・ハイグレードの具体的な車種はご指定いただけません。
  • Standard, Minivan, and High-Grade operate 24 hours. Jumbo (Hiace) has restricted operating hours for Kansai only — Kyoto → Kansai pick-up between 4:00–17:00, Kansai → Kyoto meet time between 7:00–20:00. Itami has no such restriction.
    ジャンボ車両は関西空港便のみ運行時間に制限があります（京都→関空：4:00〜17:00のお迎え、関空→京都：7:00〜20:00の待ち合わせ）。伊丹空港は制限なく全便対応です。
  • MK is not liable for delays caused by traffic, weather, or other events beyond our control.
    交通渋滞・悪天候・天災等による損害の補償はございません。
  • Cancellation policy: No fee up to 3 days before the travel date. Cancellations after that will be charged 100% of the fee.
